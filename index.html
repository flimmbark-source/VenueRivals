VENUE RIVALS

1v1 Simultaneous House Prototype

How to Play (Prototype)

Match Flow

3 rounds total. Each live round lasts 90 seconds. Between rounds there is a short 18-second shop/loadout break.

Core Loop

  1. Both players act at the same time in real time.
  2. Tap guests from your queue to admit them into your 8-slot house board.
  3. Each admission costs Turn Capacity; capacity regenerates over time.
  4. Overflow ejects the rightmost guest.

Win Condition

Highest total score after round 3 wins.
