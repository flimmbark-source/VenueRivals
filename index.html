How to Play

Goal

Play a 3-round competitive push-your-luck showdown. Score more total round points than your rival to win.

Shared Live Round Flow

  1. Both players act at the same time — each decides whether to admit another guest, activate an ability, or close the door.
  2. Push your luck — admitted guests add pressure to your house.
  3. Close to bank points — once you close, your round is locked.
  4. Bust risk — if pressure exceeds your limit before closing, you bust and lose that round.

Real-Time Rival Pressure

Guest abilities can disrupt the opponent while their round is still live, lowering limits or adding pressure.

Strategy Tips

  • Close early for a safe score, or push for a bigger round.
  • Use abilities to stabilize your house or disrupt your rival.
  • Watch your remaining pressure space before admitting again.
  • Win over 3 rounds by managing risk better than your opponent.
