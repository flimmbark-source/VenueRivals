How to Play

Match Structure

3 rounds total. Every round has a shared live Guest Phase followed by a shared Buy Phase.

Guest Phase (Parallel Turns)

  1. Both players resolve their own door guest at the same time.
  2. For each arrival choose: Admit, Activate, or Close Door.
  3. Admitted guests shift through your house lane and can push guests out the Exit door.
  4. If danger goes above your cap before closing, you bust and lose round points.

Money vs Points

💵 Money is spent in Buy Phase to improve future rounds. ⭐ Points are your win score and are never spent.

Buy Phase

Spend money on venue-linked guests. Purchased guests are shuffled into future draws, not the current guest phase.
