How to Play

The Goal

Score the most points over 3 rounds. Each round has a Guest Phase followed by a Buy Phase.

Guest Phase

Guests arrive at your door one at a time. For each guest, you choose:

  • Admit — Let them in. Earn their money & points, but take their heat.
  • Activate Ability — Use their special power instead of admitting them.
  • Close Door — Stop safely and bank what you earned.

If your heat exceeds the threshold, you BUST and lose most of your round earnings!

Buy Phase

Spend money earned during the Guest Phase to buy new guests for future rounds. Points are your score — they're never spent.

Strategy Tips

  • Watch your heat — push too far and you'll bust
  • Use abilities strategically to manage heat or disrupt your rival
  • Buy guests that complement your venue's strengths
  • Watch your opponent — their decisions play out live
