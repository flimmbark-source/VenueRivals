VENUERIVALS

Outplay. Outbuild. Outshine.

How to Play

Goal

Become the most popular venue on the block! Reach $10,000 in total earnings or drive your rival out of business to win.

Each Turn

  1. Choose an Action — Upgrade your venue, adjust pricing, hire staff, or run a promotion.
  2. Customers Arrive — They choose which venue to visit based on quality, price, reputation, and ambiance.
  3. Earn Revenue — Customers spend money at your venue. Better venues earn more per customer.
  4. Pay Costs — Staff wages and maintenance come out of your earnings.

Upgrades

Invest in your venue to attract more customers: better decor, live music, premium drinks, expanded seating, and more!

Strategy Tips

  • Balance spending on upgrades vs. saving cash reserves
  • Watch what your rival does and counter their strategy
  • Promotions give a short-term boost but cost money
  • Reputation builds slowly but is powerful long-term
